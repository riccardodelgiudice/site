Today, I attended DevFest for the first time—an event organized by the Google Developer Group.

![The DevFest Venezia attendee badge, T-shirt and tote bag](assets/blog/devfest-2026-gadgets.jpeg "The DevFest Venezia attendee kit.")

It was a valuable opportunity to discover interesting companies and professional paths, but the most meaningful part was meeting people who share similar passions and ambitions.

### Different perspectives, shared interests

Despite having common interests, everyone brought a different perspective. Our conversations gave me new ideas and reflections that I believe will be useful throughout my journey—not only as a student, but also for my personal growth.

### A welcoming community

The atmosphere was warm and welcoming, and the event was exceptionally well organized.

![Two friends attending DevFest Venezia](assets/blog/devfest-2026-with-friend.jpeg "Sharing my first DevFest experience with a friend.")

A special thank you to the Google Developer Group team and to Ca’ Foscari University of Venice for making this experience possible. I left the event feeling inspired, curious, and even more motivated to keep learning and connecting with the community.
