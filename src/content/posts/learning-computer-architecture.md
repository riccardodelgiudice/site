Computer architecture is giving me a more concrete picture of what software asks a machine to do. Concepts such as memory, instructions, and data representation stop being abstract once you see how they interact.

## Why the lower level matters

Understanding the layers below an application makes performance, constraints, and design choices easier to reason about. It also turns many “magic” abstractions into systems that can be examined step by step.

These are early notes, not final conclusions. I’ll keep updating the blog as the picture becomes clearer.
