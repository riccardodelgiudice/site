I started the Information Technology bachelor’s degree at Ca’ Foscari with a clear goal: build strong fundamentals before deciding where to specialize.

## What I’m focusing on

Right now, I’m interested in the relationship between software and the machine that runs it. Studying both computer science and computer architecture helps connect ideas that can otherwise feel separate.

- Learn consistently, one concept at a time
- Turn theory into small working projects
- Keep notes that make difficult ideas easier to revisit

This blog is where I’ll document that process: what I learn, what I build, and the questions that remain open.
