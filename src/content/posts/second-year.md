Hi, I'm Riccardo, an Information Technology student at Ca’ Foscari University of Venice.

This is the beginning of my personal blog, a place where I want to document my journey, share what I learn, and explore ideas that interest me.

### Reflections
I've just started my second year at university.
I'm very excited because I have so many interesting projects I'd like to work on to improve my knowledge and develop my skills.

But honestly, I'm also worried about the future of AI. Sometimes, I feel like everything I'm doing might be pointless. I think this is a concern shared by many aspiring programmers of my generation.

It's difficult to find the right path in all this uncertainty and chaos. However, I believe the solution is to keep building a solid foundation in computer science while gradually specialising in a particular field.

Hard work and determination will always pay off.

### JEVE Applications

A few days ago, I applied to JEVE, a student-run Junior Enterprise.

I decided to apply because I wanted to step outside my comfort zone, challenge myself, and meet new people who share my ambitions and interests.

I hope to become a member of JEVE after successfully completing the selection process. I'm convinced that this experience would help me grow in many ways, both personally and professionally.
